      How this is worked out

      Change per ten years is a Theil-Sen slope. "Clear" means a Mann-Kendall test finds it unlikely to be chance (p under 0.05). Rain measures use satellite years only, as a trend across the change of rain source would mean nothing.

      What it cannot tell you

        Satellite and ground

        Satellites see every field every few days but only broadly: NASA's weather and soil moisture cover squares tens of kilometres wide, and the top of the soil more than the root zone. A rain gauge or soil probe is exact, but only at one spot. They are strongest together: a soil test or a season of your own rain records beats our defaults, so enter yours where you can.
